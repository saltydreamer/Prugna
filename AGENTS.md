# Prugna conventions

## Stack

- Laravel 13 + Fortify (headless auth) + Spatie Permission + Inertia v3 + Vue 3 (`<script setup>`, plain JS) + Vuetify 4 + Ziggy. Database: MariaDB (SQLite in-memory for tests).
- UI copy is Italian. Server-side strings go through `__()`; validation messages live in `lang/it`.

## Authorization

- Permissions are defined ONLY in `App\Enums\Permission`; system roles in `App\Enums\Role`. After adding a case run `php artisan permissions:sync` (also called by the seeders).
- `super-admin` owns every permission through `Gate::before` in `AppServiceProvider`, but policies (`UserPolicy`, `RolePolicy`) still apply their structural rules (no self-delete, system roles not deletable, last super admin protected).
- Authorize in controllers with `$this->authorize()` / Form Request `authorize()`; the frontend uses `usePermissions().can()` only to hide UI.

## Notifications

- Every in-app notification extends `App\Notifications\AppNotification` and implements `title()` / `message()` (optionally `icon()`, `color()`, `actionUrl()`, `actionLabel()`). It is stored in the database and also mailed when the user enabled `email_notifications`.
- Notifications are queued (`ShouldQueue`): a worker must run (`composer dev` starts one).

## Registry (Anagrafiche)

- `Organization` (soft deletes = "archiviata", read-only until restored) has many `Location` (sedi) and `Contact` (contatti). Routes under `registry.*`; locations/contacts are nested and scoped (`scopeBindings`).
- Exactly one primary location/contact per organization, enforced by `App\Models\Concerns\HasPrimaryFlag` (first record is primary, deleting the primary promotes another).
- Italian fiscal data: `App\Rules\ItalianVatNumber` / `ItalianTaxCode` validate check digits; normalize with their `normalize()` before storing.
- Customers and suppliers are separate registries: `Organization::role` (`OrganizationRole` customer|supplier, never both; changeable only while no document refers to it, `hasDocuments()`). Lists/create pages at `registry.customers.*` and `registry.suppliers.*` (same `OrganizationController`, role from the route default); show/edit/nested routes stay `registry.organizations.*`. Pickers use `organizationOptions(OrganizationRole)`; sales documents validate `role = customer`, supplier orders `role = supplier`.
- Permissions `organizations.*` (customers) and `suppliers.*` (suppliers), chosen by `OrganizationRole::permission()`; `OrganizationPolicy` also covers locations and contacts (update on the organization).
- Nested API resources returned inside another resource must be `->resolve()`d, otherwise Inertia wraps them in `data`.

## Catalog (Prodotti e servizi)

- `Product` (type product/service, unit `UnitOfMeasure`, optional `BillingPeriod` for recurring services only), `ProductCategory`, `VatRate` (0% rates need a FatturaPA `nature` N1–N7 and a legal reference), `CustomerPrice` (fixed price OR discount % per organization, optional expiry).
- Always compute the price for a customer with `App\Actions\Catalog\ResolvePrice` (agreed price > agreed discount > list price; expired agreements ignored).
- Money: decimal(12,4) columns cast to strings; the frontend formats with `formatMoney()` from `@/utils/format`.
- Codes are generated by `Product::nextCode()` (P00001 / S00001) when left empty.
- Large option lists for dialogs are `Inertia::optional()` props loaded with `router.reload({ only: [...] })` when the dialog opens.

## Settings

- `App\Support\Settings` (scoped singleton, cached): key/value settings edited in Impostazioni (`company.*`, `mail.*`, `quotes.*`, `fiscal.*`). `mail.password` is stored encrypted and never sent to the frontend.
- SMTP from the UI is applied by `App\Support\MailSettings` when the mail manager is resolved; saving restarts queue workers.
- `PaymentTerm` (FatturaPA method MPxx, days, end of month); organizations have a default `payment_term_id`.

## Quotes (Preventivi)

- `Quote` + `QuoteLine` (types item/section/text, optional lines counted only when selected). Always save through `App\Actions\Quotes\SaveQuote` (numbering PRV-00001, customer snapshot, totals) and compute totals with `QuoteTotals` (mirrored in `resources/js/utils/quoteTotals.js`).
- Status flow: draft → sent → accepted/rejected; sent quotes past `valid_until` become expired via `quotes:check-expiry` (scheduled daily, also reminds owners 3 days before). Only drafts are editable/deletable; accepted quotes are final.
- Visibility: owners see their own quotes; `quotes.supervise` sees everyone's (`Quote::visibleTo()`, `QuotePolicy`).
- Hour packages: a quote item line with `package_hours` (and optional `package_months` of validity; editor `QuoteLinesEditor` with `allow-packages`) becomes an hour contract of the customer when the quote is accepted, by us or online (`App\Actions\Quotes\CreateHourPackages`, once per line: `service_contracts.quote_line_id` unique; only selected lines). The contract has `quote_id`: it is invoiced with the quote or its order, never on its own (`ServiceContract::isBilled()`); the interventions use it up as usual. Articles sold as prepaid hours: `products.hour_package` («Monte ore prepagato», services sold by the hour, optional `package_months`): `SaveQuote` / `SaveOrder` make such a line a package of as many hours as its quantity. Order lines carry `package_hours` / `package_months` too (order editor with `allow-packages`); `App\Actions\Orders\SyncOrderHourPackages` (run by `SaveOrder`) keeps one contract per package line (`service_contracts.order_id` + `order_line_id`, re-pointed by position because lines are replaced on save; invoiced with the order, `isBilled()`; new contracts start on the order date and use up at once the customer's interventions still to invoice; a removed line deletes its unused contract; cancelling the order deactivates them, deleting it releases them). An order made from an accepted quote adopts the quote's contracts. Orders saved before or imported: «Crea i contratti monte ore» on the order page (`sales.orders.hour-packages.store`, `missing()`). The order page lists the packages of the order and of its quote (`hourPackages`).
- Customers answer on the public page `/q/{public_token}` (no login, rate limited); the owner gets `QuoteActivityNotification`.
- PDF: `App\Support\QuotePdf` + `resources/views/pdf/quote.blade.php` (dompdf, DejaVu Sans with font subsetting). Line tables (`.lines`, also `pdf/partials/lines.blade.php`) have fixed column widths and wrap long words/links; options left out of the total get their own box (options, their VAT, total with the options).

## Orders and invoices (Ordini e Fatture)

- Shared document infrastructure: `DocumentTotals` (line rounding + VAT per rate), `SyncDocumentLines` (replace lines freezing VAT), `DocumentNumber::next()` (yearly numbers `ORD-2026-0001`, `FT-2026-0001`, `NC-2026-0001`, row lock + unique index), `CustomerSnapshot`, `IsDocumentLine`.
- `Order` + `OrderLine` (`quantity_invoiced`): created from an accepted quote with `SaveOrder::fromQuote()` (one per quote) or by hand. Customer and lines are editable only while nothing is invoiced (`Order::isEditable()`); `refreshInvoicedQuantities()` recomputes from invoices (drafts included) minus issued credit notes. Permissions `orders.view` / `orders.manage`; PDF "Conferma d'ordine" via `OrderPdf`.
- `Invoice` + `InvoiceLine` (types invoice TD01 / deposit TD02 / credit_note TD04, series FT/NC). Build drafts with `BuildInvoice` (from quote: full/deposit; from order: full/selection/deposit; credit note), save with `SaveInvoice` (freezes regime, withholding and pension fund from `fiscal.*`, drops split payment in VAT-free regimes, keeps the "Rimborso imposta di bollo" system line in sync unless the invoice has `charge_stamp_duty` off — the duty stays due and declared in `DatiBollo`, only the charge to the customer goes; credit notes copy the choice), issue with `IssueInvoice` (`problems()` lists what blocks issuing; numbering only at issue time, chronological order enforced). Issued invoices never change: correct them with a credit note (`BuildInvoice::creditNote()` copies lines, order, CIG/CUP and customer references; the XML repeats CIG/CUP in `DatiFattureCollegate`, where public bodies look for them; the SDI card offers it when a public body refuses the invoice). Only an issued invoice that never reached the SDI (no transmission, or only failed sends or sends to the Openapi sandbox; not imported) and is the last of its series and year can be deleted (`App\Actions\Invoices\DeleteIssuedInvoice`, `problems()` shown in the page): its number is reused and what it billed goes back to be invoiced. Test invoices (sent only to the sandbox) can be deleted even when not the last.
- Fiscal totals: `InvoiceCalculator` (pension fund on the taxable, withholding, split payment, virtual stamp duty > 77.47 €), mirrored in `resources/js/utils/invoiceTotals.js`. Deposits are deducted in the balance as negative system lines (`deducts_invoice_id`), only once issued.
- FatturaPA XML: `App\Support\FatturaPa\FatturaPaXml` (FPR12/FPA12; public bodies need the 6 character office code, CIG/CUP go in `DatiOrdineAcquisto`, Openapi signs FPA12 invoices automatically). Strings must be Basic Latin/Latin-1 (`text()` transliterates). `FatturaPaXmlTest` validates against the official XSD in `tests/Fixtures/fatturapa`.
- Permissions `invoices.view`, `invoices.manage` (drafts), `invoices.issue` (issue, credit notes). PDF courtesy copy via `InvoicePdf` (`resources/views/pdf/invoice.blade.php`, all PDFs extend `pdf/layout.blade.php`).

## Service contracts (Contratti servizi)

- `Subscription` (numbering `CS-2026-0001`, customer, `BillingPeriod`, `billing_mode` advance|arrears, `starts_on`, optional `ends_on`, `billed_until` for fees invoiced outside Prugna, payment term, `is_active`) + `SubscriptionLine` (services only, saved with `SyncDocumentLines`, editor `QuoteLinesEditor` with `items-only`) + `SubscriptionPeriod` (fee put in an invoice; FK `invoice_id` cascade: deleting the invoice makes the fee due again). Periods follow one another from `billingStart()` (the day after `billed_until`, else `starts_on`; `period()`, `addMonthsNoOverflow`), so a contract invoiced elsewhere up to a date resumes the day after; the start date can't follow that day (`SubscriptionRequest::after()`, the form moves it). A fee is due when its period starts (advance) or ends (arrears) — `duePeriods()`, `nextPeriod()`, `periodLabel()` («novembre 2026», «anno 2027», «dal … al …»). Customer, start, `billed_until` and period are frozen once a fee is invoiced (`SubscriptionRequest::scheduleEditable()`).
- «Genera fatture da servizi»: `App\Actions\Subscriptions\GenerateSubscriptionInvoices` — `preview(date)` (fees due by the date, grouped by customer, keyed `subscription id:period start`), `handle(date, keys, owner)` (one draft per customer and payment term, a section per contract when there are several, one line per service and period «… – canone novembre 2026», VAT through `RegimeVatRate`). Drafts only: the user checks and issues them. Routes `sales.subscriptions.*`, `sales.subscriptions.due` (JSON preview), `sales.subscriptions.generate`; UI `SubscriptionInvoiceDialog.vue` (list page and contract page). The invoice page links back to its contracts.
- Permissions `subscriptions.view` / `subscriptions.manage`; generating also needs `invoices.manage` (`SubscriptionPolicy::invoice`). Contracts with invoiced fees can't be deleted (suspend them or set an end date).

## Preventivatore (experimental)

- `Estimate` (numbering `PS-2026-0001`, optional customer, `months` default 36, general `markup_percent`, `category_markups` {category: %}, `upfront`, `starts_on`, AI-drafted `advantages` one per line, `status` draft/accepted, `subscription_id`) + `EstimateItem` (free `category`, description, `url`, quantity, `unit_cost`: VAT included in VAT-free regimes, excluded otherwise; `note` from the AI). Saved with `SaveEstimate`; totals by `App\Support\Estimates\EstimateCalculator` (price = cost × (1 + markup of the category), monthly = (price − upfront) / months), mirrored in `resources/js/utils/estimateTotals.js`. Permission `estimates.manage`; routes `sales.estimates.*`.
- `App\Support\Ai\LinkReader`: downloads the product page (public addresses only, also after redirects), JSON-LD/meta price + visible text → `OpenRouterClient::structured()` (text input, JSON schema) → name and cost (VAT handled by regime); without AI only the structured price. Amazon links (`BLOCKING_HOSTS`) are never downloaded: they suggest the screenshot. `readScreenshot()` (route `sales.estimates.read-image`, needs `ai.use`): PNG/JPG/WebP pasted, dropped or chosen in the item dialog, shrunk with `DocumentReader::shrink()` (2400 px) → `OpenRouterClient::extract()`, same schema and result; the optional link is kept on the item. `GenerateAdvantages` drafts the advantages. Report `EstimatePdf` (`pdf/estimate.blade.php`: included items by category, plan by year, advantages; never costs or markups). `AcceptEstimate`: monthly `Subscription` for the term (one «Canone» line) + draft invoice of the upfront; the estimate is then read-only.

## Purchases (Acquisti) and rebilled costs

- `PurchaseOrder` + `PurchaseOrderLine` (numbering `OF-2026-0001`, statuses draft/sent/confirmed/received/cancelled), saved with `SavePurchaseOrder`. Optional `order_id` (customer order; forces `customer_id` to its customer) and `customer_id` (who the costs are charged to). Lines are at cost; `rebill` + `markup_percent` mark costs to charge back (ignored without a customer). Permissions `purchases.view` / `purchases.manage`. PDF via `PurchaseOrderPdf` (never shows customer or markup); email via the generic `App\Mail\DocumentMail` + `DocumentSendDialog.vue`.
- Rebilling: `App\Actions\Purchases\RebillCosts` — `available()` (cost lines of the customer/order not yet invoiced, any "rebill" flag, non-zero; supplier orders only when sent/confirmed/received), `pending()` (only the ones marked "rebill", proposed by `include_costs` when invoicing an order), `resolve(keys)`, `lines(costs, markups, grouped, description, listDetails)` (one line per cost, or a single line per VAT rate). Costs are keyed `po-ID` / `ex-ID`; an invoice line lists them in `costs` and `SaveInvoice` stores them in `invoice_line_costs` (`InvoiceLineCost`, one line → many costs). Endpoints: `sales.invoices.lookup.costs`, `sales.invoices.lookup.cost-lines` (editor), `sales.invoices.from-costs` (draft invoice); UI `CostInvoiceDialog.vue` (order, expense, invoice editor). `InvoiceRequest` checks customer, committed status, single use; credit notes drop the links. Cost lines already invoiced are frozen (`isEditable()`).
- Past costs (temporary, to align the data from before Prugna): «Imputa costi pregressi» on the order (`OrderPastCostsController`, `sales.orders.past-costs.*`, `PastCostsDialog.vue`, permission to create expenses) assigns paid expenses — not in another order, of the customer or of nobody, not charged back yet — to an order already invoiced with `expenses.past_cost`: they count in the order's costs and margin, `RebillCosts` never proposes them; linking an expense normally resets the flag.
- Order page shows costs and estimated margin: order revenue + invoice lines charging back its costs + costs marked to charge back − all costs.

## Expenses (Spese) and documents read by AI

- `Expense` + `ExpenseLine` (types invoice / receipt / credit_note, `AccountingCategory` (`category_id`), `PaymentMethod`, `due_date`, `paid_at`; scopes `unpaid()` / `overdue()`), saved with `SaveExpense` (supplier snapshot, lines, totals, attachment on the private `local` disk). Supplier must have role supplier; the same supplier number per year can't be recorded twice. Optional `order_id` / `customer_id` + line `rebill` / `markup_percent`: charged back like supplier order lines (`RebillCosts` handles both sources). Linking to an order/customer (single or bulk, optionally marking lines "rebill" with a markup) via `purchases.expenses.link` + `ExpenseLinkDialog.vue`; expenses already invoiced can't be relinked. Permissions `expenses.view` / `expenses.manage`.
- Document reading: `App\Support\Ai\DocumentReader` stores the upload and returns an `AiExtraction` (status, result, model, tokens, cost, time). FatturaPA XML / .p7m are parsed by `App\Support\FatturaPa\FatturaPaReader` (no AI); photos (shrunk to 2000 px) and PDFs go to `OpenRouterClient` (chat completions, `response_format` json_schema strict, `provider` with `require_parameters` and `data_collection: deny` / `zdr`, PDFs via the `file-parser` plugin). Schemas and instructions in `DocumentSchemas`; drafts built by `ExpenseDraft` / `OrganizationDraft` (supplier matched by VAT/CF, customers never used as suppliers, VAT rates mapped, total checked). Nothing is saved until the user confirms the prefilled form (`?extraction=ID`).
- Settings `ai.enabled`, `ai.api_key` (encrypted, never sent to the frontend), `ai.model` (chosen from the live OpenRouter catalog, cached 6 h), `ai.privacy`. Permission `ai.use` (XML import doesn't need it). In tests fake `openrouter.ai/*` with `Http::fake`.

## Interventions (Interventi)

- `Intervention` (numbering `INT-2026-0001` from `started_at`, saved with `SaveIntervention`): customer, optional location/contact/order, `InterventionCategory`, `InterventionMode` remote|onsite, technician, `started_at` (UTC; local times use `config('app.display_timezone')` / `localStart()`), `duration_minutes` (null = timer running), description, internal notes, `billable`; on site only: `callout`, `km`, `travel_minutes`, `InterventionMaterial` lines (remote interventions drop them). Status is derived (`InterventionStatus`: running / to_invoice / invoiced / not_billable).
- Timer: `interventions.start` (one running per user) / `interventions.stop` (duration = elapsed minutes); the running one is the shared prop `runningIntervention` (chip + stop button in the app bar, start dialog `InterventionStartDialog.vue`).
- Billing: time and travel are rounded up to `interventions.rounding_minutes` (1/15/30/60, default 30: `Intervention::roundUp()`, mirrored in `resources/js/utils/duration.js`). Each category bills hours with its remote/onsite service (`ResolvePrice`, so customer prices apply); call-out, km and travel use the services in `interventions.{callout,km,travel}_product_id`. `App\Actions\Interventions\BillInterventions` builds the lines (one set per intervention, or grouped per category+mode with extras on their own lines); every line lists its `interventions` ids, stored by `SaveInvoice` in `invoice_line_interventions`. `InvoiceRequest` checks customer, completed, billable and single use. Invoiced interventions (credit notes excluded) are frozen. Endpoints `sales.invoices.lookup.interventions`, `sales.invoices.lookup.intervention-lines` (editor), `sales.invoices.from-interventions`; UI `InterventionInvoiceDialog.vue` (list, intervention, order and customer pages, invoice editor).
- Rapportino: `App\Support\InterventionPdf` (`resources/views/pdf/intervention.blade.php`, never prints internal notes or prices), routes `interventions.pdf` / `compose` / `send` (generic `DocumentMail`, sets `report_sent_at`). Customer signature: `SignatureDialog.vue` (canvas) posts a PNG data URL to `interventions.sign` (validated PNG ≤ 512 KB, stored on the private `local` disk); signed interventions are final (`isEditable()`), supervisors can remove the signature (`interventions.unsign`).
- Split: `App\Actions\Interventions\SplitIntervention` turns an intervention whose description lists several dated jobs (the Dolibarr import writes «30/04/2026 14:30 · 4 h 30 min · text», one per Dolibarr line) into one intervention per job (`interventions.split`, banner on the page with `splitCount`): the original keeps the first job, the on-site extras and the materials; the others are new (INT-…, `split_from_id`, same customer/category/mode/technician, header and notes in the internal notes). Not for running, invoiced or signed interventions. The Dolibarr import never updates an intervention split in Prugna.
- Invoiced outside Prugna: `interventions.invoiced_outside_at` (e.g. invoiced in Dolibarr before switching) counts as invoiced (`isInvoicedOutsidePrugna()`, `isInvoiced()`, status Invoiced, excluded from `toInvoice()`, frozen, rejected by `InvoiceRequest`). Bulk «Segna come già fatturati» up to a local day with `App\Actions\Interventions\MarkInterventionsInvoiced` (`candidates()` flags interventions listing jobs after that day: split them first) via `interventions.invoiced-outside.index` (JSON preview) / `.store`, UI `InvoicedOutsideDialog.vue` on the list; `.destroy` unmarks one from its page. Policy `markInvoiced` / `unmarkInvoiced` (`invoices.manage` + `interventions.supervise`).
- Hour contracts: `ServiceContract` (numbering `MO-2026-0001`, hours, validity dates compared as calendar days, optional mode and `category_ids`, price invoiced once with `interventions.contracts.invoice`, manager). `ApplyContract` (called by `SaveIntervention` and the timer stop) takes the rounded minutes from the first covering contract with hours left (`contract_id`, `contract_minutes`, `covered` when nothing is left to invoice; `skip_contract` opts out); the rest is invoiced (`invoiceableMinutes()`). `ContractHoursNotification` to the manager at 80% and when exhausted (once each; reset when hours change). Permission `contracts.manage` (supervisors can consult).
- Settings page `interventions.settings.*` (permission `settings.manage`): categories (services can be created on the fly from a price), rounding, extra services. Permissions `interventions.view` / `interventions.manage` (own interventions), `interventions.supervise` (everyone's, `Intervention::visibleTo()`).

## Electronic invoicing (SDI via Openapi)

- `App\Support\Sdi\OpenapiClient`: hosts `test.sdi.openapi.it` / `sdi.openapi.it` (`sdi.environment`), bearer token created from `sdi.username` + `sdi.api_key` (Basic auth on `oauth.openapi.it/token`, only the SDI scopes listed by `/scopes`), cached encrypted in `sdi.token` and renewed on 401. Every call is logged in `openapi_calls` with its price from `sdi.prices` (`OpenapiClient::prices()`: endpoint like `POST /invoices`, ids normalized to `{id}` → `{price, free}`; free calls per day as in the Openapi price list; sandbox and failed calls cost 0) → cost page `settings.einvoicing.costs`.
- `ConnectSdi` registers the company (`business_registry_configurations`, no signature, no legal storage: conservation stays with the Agenzia delle Entrate) and the callbacks (`api_configurations`, events customer-invoice / customer-notification / supplier-invoice / invoice-status-quarantena / invoice-status-invoice-error; a new configuration replaces the previous one; status callbacks have no `event` key, so each callback URL carries `?event=`) to `webhooks.openapi.sdi` with `Authorization: Bearer {sdi.webhook_secret}`. Webhook routes live in `routes/webhooks.php` (no web middleware); events are stored in `sdi_events` and applied by the queued `ProcessSdiEvent`.
- Sending: `SendInvoiceToSdi` (on issue when `sdi.auto_send`, or `sales.invoices.sdi.send`) creates an `SdiTransmission` per attempt with its own ProgressivoInvio (`FatturaPaXml::transmissionProgressive()`, "S…"); `SdiStatus` from the notifications (RC delivered, MC not delivered, NS rejected with errors → can be sent again with refreshed customer data, NE accepted/refused, DT); `invoices.sdi_status` mirrors the latest one. `SdiOutcomeNotification` to the owner on rejected/refused/not delivered.
- Openapi knows the company by its VAT number (`sdi.fiscal_id`, set by «Collega»): when the company VAT changes afterwards `OpenapiClient::fiscalIdMismatch()` blocks sending (manual and on issue) and the settings page, the SDI card and the company save ask to connect again. «Missing configuration for fiscal Id» from Openapi gets the same explanation. Connecting again replaces the registration made with the previous VAT number (its callbacks too): Openapi accepts each email only once.
- `invoices.sdi_external`: transmitted outside Prugna, never sent from here. `sales.invoices.sdi.external` (policies `markToSend` / `markSentOutside`) toggles it; until the first transmission the customer data are the current ones (`SendInvoiceToSdi` refreshes the snapshot on the first send and after a rejection); `IssueInvoice::sendProblems()` (company, customer, lines) blocks incomplete invoices.
- Received: `ReceivedInvoice` inbox (`purchases.received.*`): supplier-invoice callbacks (XML downloaded from `invoices_download`) or XML/.p7m/ZIP imported from "Fatture e Corrispettivi" (`ImportReceivedInvoice`, deduplicated by hash/uuid, `_MT_` metadata skipped). "Registra spesa" creates an `AiExtraction` from the XML and opens the prefilled expense; saving it marks the invoice recorded. Openapi's recipient code `JKKZDGR` must be registered on the AdE portal.
- Official spec: https://console.openapi.com/oas/it/sdi.openapi.json. `GET /invoices/{uuid}` gives the `marking` (`SdiStatus::fromMarking()`), used by «Aggiorna esito» with the notifications; `GET /invoices?type=1` (paginated, `hasMore`) recovers received invoices («Recupera dallo SDI»). Sandbox: `POST /simulate/customer-notification` ({uuid, notification}) and `/simulate/supplier-invoice` (JSON invoice, `SimulateSupplierInvoice`), from the UI or `php artisan sdi:sandbox-test` (workflow "SDI sandbox test").
- `php artisan sdi:diagnose` (workflow "SDI diagnostics") prints configuration, scopes, registry, callbacks and the latest transmissions/events/calls without secrets or contents. In tests fake `*.openapi.it/*` with `Http::fake`.

## Installable app, push notifications and offline field work

- PWA: `PwaController` serves `/manifest.webmanifest` and `/sw.js` (from `resources/js/sw.js`, with `__VERSION__` = hash of the Vite manifest and `__ASSETS__` = built js/css/woff2 to precache). Icons in `public/icons`, offline fallback `public/offline.html`. The worker is registered in production builds only (`resources/js/utils/pwa.js`, also the install prompt and push helpers). Pages in `OFFLINE_PAGES` (`/field`) are cached network-first (HTML and Inertia JSON apart); the layout posts "warm" to refresh them and "clear" on logout. Worker requests carry `X-SW-Warm`: guests get a 401, never a login redirect or an "intended" URL.
- Push: `laravel-notification-channels/webpush`; VAPID keys generated on first use and stored in the settings (`App\Support\WebPushKeys`, applied before the channel resolves). Every `AppNotification` also goes to `WebPushChannel` when the user has `pushSubscriptions` (`toWebPush()`: title, body, icon, `data.url`). Devices subscribe from Profilo → Notifiche (`profile.push.*`, test notification `PushTestNotification`). The channel needs ext-bcmath or gmp (Docker image and CI have bcmath).
- Field work offline: `field.index` (`FieldController`) carries customers with sites, categories, products; `resources/js/utils/fieldQueue.js` keeps the interventions recorded on the device in IndexedDB (per user) and syncs them to `field.sync` (`SyncInterventionRequest`: same rules as the form, `client_uuid` makes it idempotent, optional `signature` {name, data} via `SignIntervention`, materials without a price get the customer price). Local timer in localStorage. The layout syncs on load, when the connection returns and every minute, and shows offline/pending badges.

## Dolibarr import

- Page `settings.dolibarr.*` (permission `dolibarr.import`): upload of Dolibarr's database backup (.sql/.gz/.zip) → queued `RunDolibarrImport` (simulation first, then the real import; the backup is deleted after importing). A job claims its run (`started_at`), so a second delivery does nothing.
- `App\Support\Dolibarr\SqlDumpReader` parses only CREATE TABLE / INSERT of `DolibarrSource::TABLES` into a SQLite staging database (the dump is never executed). `App\Actions\Dolibarr\ImportDolibarr` runs the steps (`ImportThirdParties`, `ImportCatalog`, `ImportSales`, `ImportPurchases`, `ImportInterventions`) in one transaction: a simulation rolls it back, so its report (`ImportReport`) is exactly what the import does.
- Repeatable: `dolibarr_links` (source like `societe.customer`, `facture` + Dolibarr id → record) via `DolibarrMapper::linked()` / `remember()`; existing records are matched by VAT/tax code (organizations), code (products), supplier + number + year (expenses, Prugna's version kept). Third parties both customer and supplier become two organizations. Users are matched by email, never created.
- Issued invoices keep Dolibarr's number; its trailing digits are the sequence of (series, year), so Prugna's numbering continues. Numbers already used by Prugna invoices are blocking errors. Imported issued invoices get `invoices.sdi_external` (transmitted outside Prugna: `Invoice::isSentOutsidePrugna()`, never sent — policy `sendToSdi`, `SdiCard`); importing again keeps a later choice and never touches invoices sent from Prugna.
- Imported values bypass mass assignment (`forceCreate` / `forceFill`); amounts are kept as Dolibarr computed them (credit notes are stored negative in Dolibarr and turned positive). Tests: `tests/Fixtures/dolibarr/backup.sql`.

## Accounting (Contabilità)

- Cash basis (as the flat-rate regime). `AccountingCategory` (user's categories replacing the old fixed expense categories, `legacy_key` = old value; `kind` cost / revenue / tax / contribution, default `business_share`), `BusinessLine` (lines of business: revenue through `products.business_line_id` of the invoice lines, costs through `expenses.business_line_id` and movements), `FinancialAccount` (bank / card / cash, `opening_balance` at `opening_date`: `balance()` counts only movements from that day, so recovered history doesn't change it), `AccountMovement` (signed amount; `invoice_id` = collection/refund, `expense_id` = payment, `transfer_uuid` = both sides of a transfer, else a manual entry with category and line). Permissions `accounting.view` / `accounting.manage`.
- Expenses: `category_id`, `business_line_id`, `business_share` (default from the category); paying with an account (`SyncExpensePayment`, from the payment dialog or the form) keeps one movement in step with `paid_at`. Invoices: `paid_amount` / `paid_at` refreshed from the movements (`Invoice::refreshPayments()`), `outstanding()` subtracts issued credit notes (`creditedAmount()`); collections recorded from the invoice page (`accounting.collections.store`, `RecordInvoiceCollection`, `InvoiceCollectionsCard.vue`). `ImportDolibarrCollections` turns the «Incassi registrati in Dolibarr:» lines of the imported invoices' notes into collections (button in Cassa e banca, idempotent).
- `App\Support\Accounting\ProfitAndLoss::forYear()`: revenue = collections × subtotal / amount_due (no VAT, withholding added back, credit notes negative) + other revenue movements; costs = expenses by `paid_at` (total in VAT-free regimes, subtotal otherwise) × business share + manual movements by category; taxes and contributions below the operating result; movements without category and transfers excluded (counted as unclassified). `FlatRateTaxEstimate` (RF19 only): collected × coefficient − contributions paid → imposta sostitutiva; INPS gestione separata (% of gross income) or artigiani/commercianti (fixed + % above the minimum, optional 35% reduction); parameters `accounting.*` in Settings. Pages `accounting.index` (Conto economico), `accounting.cash.*` (Cassa e banca: accounts, movements, transfers, receivables via `Receivables`), `accounting.settings.*` (Categorie e attività, tax parameters).

## Statistics (Statistiche)

- `statistics.index` (`StatisticsController`, permission `statistics.view`): revenue by year, month and customer from `App\Support\RevenueStatistics` — issued invoices (imported ones too) by invoice date, taxable line totals without the stamp duty charged back, credit notes subtracted. A year in progress is compared with the same period of the previous one.
- Charts are plain SVG (`resources/js/components/charts/ColumnChart.vue`, no chart library): thin columns, accent for the subject and theme gray for context, tooltip on hover/focus, a table view next to every chart.
